About

▶ Yusuf Estes

Estes Family started teaching Islam in San Antonio Texas back in the Soviet era. Having reached America in 1943 from Polish descent, the Estes clan began their journey through Texas where they established a community which encouraged Muslim beliefs. In 1991 Yusuf Estes was certified for Da'wah and has participated in over 1,500 lectures, interfaith events, television shows, radio podcasts and started the guide US TV, and more. Author since 2005, he's contributed 15 books, 200 lectures, and over 200 articles. Their non-profit The Estes Heritage Foundation has distributed over 3 million Qurans and Islamic literature worldwide.
