What is The Quran?

The Quran is the central religious text of Islam, believed by Muslims to be a revelation from God (Allah). It is widely regarded as the finest work in classical Arabic literature and has been a source of guidance for over 1,400 years.

The Quran addresses humanity with guidance for all aspects of life - spiritual, moral, social, and legal. It contains 114 chapters (surahs) and was revealed to the Prophet Muhammad (peace be upon him) over a period of approximately 23 years.

Muslims believe the Quran was verbally revealed from Allah to Muhammad through the angel Gabriel, gradually over a period of approximately 23 years, beginning on 22 December 609 CE, when Muhammad was 40, and concluding in 632 CE, the year of his death.

What's Islam?

Islam is a monotheistic Abrahamic religion that teaches that there is only one God (Allah) and that Muhammad is a messenger of God. It is the world's second-largest religion with over 1.8 billion followers or 24% of the world's population, most commonly known as Muslims.

Muslims believe that Islam is the complete and universal version of a primordial faith that was revealed many times before through prophets including Adam, Abraham, Moses, and Jesus. The Quran, the holy book of Islam, was revealed to Muhammad and is considered the verbatim word of God.

The Five Pillars of Islam are the foundation of Muslim life: declaration of faith (shahada), prayer (salat), giving to charity (zakat), fasting during Ramadan (sawm), and pilgrimage to Mecca (hajj).

Who is Allah?

Allah is the Arabic word for God. It is used by Muslims and Arabic-speaking Christians and Jews to refer to the One God. The word Allah has been used by Arabic people of different religions since pre-Islamic times.

In Islam, Allah is the unique, omnipotent and only deity and creator of the universe and is equivalent to God in other Abrahamic religions. Muslims believe that Allah is the same God worshipped by Christians and Jews - the God of Abraham, Moses, Jesus, and all the prophets.

The Islamic view emphasizes the absolute oneness and uniqueness of God (Tawhid). Allah has 99 names in the Quran that describe His attributes, such as The Merciful (Ar-Rahman), The Compassionate (Ar-Rahim), The All-Knowing (Al-Aleem), and The All-Powerful (Al-Aziz).

Bible and Quran - Understanding Both

Muslims honor and respect the Bible as one of the previous revelations from God. The Quran mentions the Torah (Tawrat), Psalms (Zabur), and Gospel (Injeel) as previous scriptures revealed by Allah to different prophets.

However, Muslims believe that the Quran is the final revelation from God, completing and perfecting the message that was revealed in previous scriptures. The Quran confirms many of the same stories and teachings found in the Bible while also providing new guidance and clarification.

Both books share many common themes: belief in one God, the importance of prayer and charity, the Day of Judgment, and stories of prophets like Abraham, Moses, and Jesus. Understanding both scriptures can provide a deeper appreciation of the Abrahamic faith tradition.

Who Is Yusuf Estes?

Sheikh Yusuf Estes is an American Islamic scholar and preacher who converted from Christianity to Islam in 1991. Born in 1944 in Texas, he was initially a Christian minister and businessman before his transformative journey to Islam.

Following his conversion, Yusuf Estes dedicated his life to spreading Islamic teachings through engaging lectures, videos, and online initiatives. He founded Guide US TV, a free-to-air Islamic television channel that reaches millions worldwide, making Islamic knowledge accessible to English-speaking audiences.

In 2012, he was honored as the Islamic Personality of the Year at the Dubai International Holy Quran Award. Known for his warm personality and ability to explain Islam to people from all backgrounds, Sheikh Yusuf has traveled globally, delivering lectures and promoting interfaith understanding.

The Estes Heritage Foundation continues his mission of sharing Islam through education and free distribution of Qurans and Islamic literature to seekers of truth worldwide.
